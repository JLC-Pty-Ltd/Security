Step by step

Install the app

The app is downloaded directly from this website, not from the Google Play Store, so Android asks for your permission before installing it. This is normal and takes about two minutes.

  1. Download the file

    Tap Download for Android above. Your browser may warn that This type of file can harm your device. Tap Download anyway.

  2. Open the downloaded file

    Tap Open in the download notification, or open your Files (or My Files) app, go to Downloads and tap JLC-OHS.apk.

  3. Allow installs from your browser

    If you see For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then tap the back arrow.

  4. Tap Install

    Tap Install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning such as Unsafe app blocked or asks to scan the app, tap More details → Install anyway, or allow the scan. Play Protect shows this for any app that isn't from the Play Store.

  5. Open JLC

    Tap Open, or find the gold JLC OHS crest icon on your home screen or in your app drawer.

Tip: once the app is installed, you can switch Allow from this source off again under Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps.

Getting around

How to use the app

Menu

Tap the ☰ button at the top right to jump to Services, How we work, Plans, Industries or Contact.

Services

Scroll through our seven services (A–G), from risk assessments to contractor compliance safety files, to see what each one includes.

Plans

Under Plans, tap Monthly or Annual to compare subscription prices. Annual plans include two months free.

Back button

Your phone's back button or back gesture returns to the previous page. On the first page it closes the app.

Request an assessment

  1. Open the enquiry form

    Tap Request an assessment in the menu, or go to Contact.

  2. Fill in your details

    Enter your name and email (both required), plus your company and phone number.

  3. Choose the service and plan

    Pick the service you need and whether you want a once-off job or a monthly or annual subscription. If you're not sure, choose the closest one and explain in the message.

  4. Describe your workplace

    Tell us the type of work, number of employees and location. This helps us decide whether a remote or on-site assessment suits you.

  5. Send

    Tap Send enquiry. You'll see Thank you, your enquiry has been sent. We reply within one working day.

Call or email us

Tap the phone number or email address under Company information. Your phone app or email app opens with our details filled in.

No signal?

The app needs internet. Without a connection, it shows an offline screen with our contact details and a Try again button.

Coming soon: a client area where subscribers can upload evidence, track corrective actions and get expiry reminders. It will arrive as a free update.

Keeping current

Updating the app

Most changes, like new services and prices, appear in the app automatically because it loads the latest content each time you open it. When we release a new version of the app itself, it will be listed on this page.

  1. Download the new version

    Come back to this page and tap Download for Android.

  2. Install over the old one

    Open the file and tap Update. You don't need to uninstall the old version first.

Apple devices

Using an iPhone or iPad?

APK files only work on Android. On an iPhone or iPad you can add JLC to your home screen instead, and it opens like an app:

  1. Open the website in Safari

    Go to jlc-risk-assesment.github.io/Website.

  2. Tap Share

    Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.

  3. Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Troubleshooting

If something goes wrong

"App not installed" or "Package conflicts with an existing package"
An older copy that wasn't downloaded from this page may be installed. Uninstall it, then install again. Also check that your phone has free storage.
"There was a problem parsing the package"
The download didn't finish, or your phone runs Android 5 or older. Delete the file, download it again on a stable connection, and check your Android version under Settings → About phone.
I can't find the downloaded file
Open your Files app and look in Downloads, or open your browser's menu → Downloads.
The app shows a blank or offline screen
Check your mobile data or Wi-Fi, then tap Try again.
My enquiry won't send
Call us on 081 868 0802 or email jlcriskassesment@proton.me and we'll help directly.

Your information

Privacy & safety

What the app can access

Internet only. It can't see your contacts, photos, location, camera or files.

What we receive

Only what you type into the enquiry form, and only when you tap Send. There is no account and no tracking.

Only download the app from this page. A copy from anywhere else may have been changed. To check your file, compare its SHA-256 fingerprint with ours: fafced745e9b66f11313b9bfa7080b0a3a50efdeb51154687041c7e116d90887

Removing the app

Press and hold the JLC OHS icon, then tap Uninstall, or go to Settings → Apps → JLC OHS → Uninstall.
