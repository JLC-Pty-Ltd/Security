Core services

Seven services, one safety system

From a single risk assessment to managing your compliance month after month.

A

Occupational Health & Safety Risk Assessments

Structured assessments to identify hazards, evaluate risk and recommend suitable controls.

  • Baseline, site-specific and task-based risk assessments
  • Job Safety Analysis (JSA) and activity-based assessments
  • Hazard identification, risk ratings and control measures
  • Corrective action plans, reviews and updates
B

Remote Digital Site Assessments

A guided remote process that can reduce the need for an initial site visit where the work and evidence can be assessed reliably.

  • Digital site questionnaires and document collection
  • Photographs, video evidence and live virtual walkthroughs
  • Evidence review, hazard identification and risk classification
  • Escalation to physical inspection where required
C

Comprehensive Safety File Development

Site-specific safety documentation based on your activities, legal requirements and contractor or client specifications.

  • OHS policies, legal registers and appointments
  • Risk assessments, safe work procedures and method statements
  • PPE, induction, training and competency records
  • Emergency plans, chemical registers and SDSs
D

Workplace Inspections & OHS Audits

Planned inspections and compliance reviews to identify gaps and monitor improvement.

  • Workplace, housekeeping and PPE inspections
  • Machinery and equipment safety reviews
  • Fire and emergency preparedness checks
  • Contractor compliance reviews and management reports
E

Training & Safety Awareness

Awareness support that reinforces safe behaviour and communicates site rules.

  • OHS inductions and general safety awareness
  • PPE, housekeeping and manual-handling awareness
  • Fire safety and emergency preparedness
  • Toolbox talks and incident-prevention topics
F

Ongoing Compliance Management

Continuing support to keep your OHS documentation and action plans current.

  • Scheduled compliance and safety-file reviews
  • Certificate and training-record expiry monitoring
  • Incident investigation support and risk assessment updates
  • Monthly or agreed-period compliance reports
G

Contractor Compliance Safety Files

Getting your team approved to work on a client's or principal contractor's site. We compile the contractor safety file to their specification, so you're ready for site access and the client's safety audit.

  • Company documents, COIDA Letter of Good Standing and tax compliance
  • Section 37(2) mandatary agreement
  • Legal appointments and competency certificates
  • Site-specific risk assessments and method statements
  • Fall protection plans where work at height applies
  • Medical fitness certificates and induction records
  • PPE issue registers and toolbox talk records
  • Equipment inspection registers and incident register

Remote assessment methodology

How a JLC assessment works

01

Client onboarding

Company details, site activities, employees, work processes and client-specific requirements.

02

Digital evidence collection

Questionnaires, photographs, videos, document uploads and live virtual walkthroughs.

03

Hazard & risk evaluation

Hazards, persons exposed, existing controls, likelihood, severity and additional controls.

04

Compliance & documentation review

Records reviewed, gaps identified, site-specific documents and corrective actions prepared.

05

Reporting & follow-up

Findings report, action owners and target dates, then close-out verification or a physical follow-up.

Transparent verification. Photographs and documents do not replace physical inspection, testing or specialist verification. Anything that can't be reliably verified remotely is recorded as pending verification or referred for an on-site assessment, never presented as compliant.
